Pune, located in Maharashtra on the Deccan Plateau, is a vibrant metropolis known as the "Oxford of the East" for its prestigious educational institutions and rich cultural history. Formerly the seat of the Maratha Empire under the Peshwas, the city offers a blend of historical charm, such as Shaniwar Wada, and modern IT and automotive industrial growth.

Often termed the cultural capital of Maharashtra, Pune hosts the famous Ganesh Festival and the Sawai Gandharva Music Festival. The city boasts a pleasant climate, bustling cafes, and access to stunning Sahyadri hill forts like Sinhgad.
